## What the profiles mean

`strict` means original numeric axis-aligned box geometry was retained. It does
NOT mean Source player-hull timing, restarts, settings or physics were verified.
`geometry-proposal` means a polygon/ring was enclosed by an AABB and/or a thin
positive native height was expanded to 73 units to avoid target trigger collapse.
Those are MATERIAL TIMING DIFFERENCES, not exact conversions. Per-region source
Z/emitted corners/padding are in the reports; full source polygon is in the native
copy. Do not silently adopt these into ranked timing.

Mixed stage/checkpoint routes, unsupported bonus routes, target index/cap overflow
and missing zone tables remain blocked as whole maps. They are never silently
flattened, truncated or partially emitted; native backups and reasons remain.
The JSON also cannot carry all native max velocity, global regions, speed flags,
restart yaw/destinations and trigger/physics semantics; see every map's warnings.
